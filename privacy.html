1. Who we are

Tutti is a family meal planning application operated by Lucas van Tilburg (ABN 17 403 743 343), an individual based in Victoria, Australia ("we", "us", or "our").

We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through the Tutti mobile application.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register or sign in (including via Apple Sign-In).
  • Family information — names and details of family members you add to your household within the app.
  • Dietary preferences — dietary needs and restrictions you configure for yourself or your family.
  • Recipe interactions — recipes you swipe, vote on, like, or dislike; meal plans you create or generate.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — features used, screens viewed, and interactions within the app, collected to improve the service.
  • Device information — device type, operating system version, and app version for troubleshooting purposes.
  • Purchase information — subscription status and transaction history managed through Apple's App Store and RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and family household.
  • Provide personalised meal planning and recipe recommendations based on your family's preferences and voting history.
  • Process and manage your subscription to Tutti Pro.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate service updates.
  • Improve the app through aggregated, de-identified usage analysis.
  • Comply with our legal obligations under Australian law.

We do not use your personal information for advertising, nor do we sell it to third parties.

4. How we share your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — with third-party companies that help us operate the service (see Section 5 below), under contractual obligations to protect your data.
  • Legal requirements — where required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process.
  • Business transfers — if Tutti is acquired or merged, your information may transfer to the new operator, who will remain bound by this policy.

6. Data retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or compliance purposes.

Aggregated, de-identified usage data may be retained indefinitely.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access. These include encrypted data transmission (TLS), access controls, and using reputable third-party infrastructure providers.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

Tutti is intended for use by adults managing a family household. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Family member names entered by an account holder may relate to children, but we treat all data within a family account as belonging to the account holder.

If you believe a child's information has been provided to us without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

9. Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct personal information that is inaccurate or out of date.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal information.
  • Make a complaint if you believe we have handled your information in breach of the Privacy Act.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 11. We will respond within a reasonable time, and no later than 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response to a complaint, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we will notify you via the app or by email. Continued use of Tutti after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
