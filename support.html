Frequently Asked Questions

How do I invite my family to join?

Go to the Family tab and tap your invite code to share it. Anyone who enters the code in their app will be added to your family group.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store. Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone, find Tutti, and tap Cancel Subscription.

Can I use Tutti without a family?

Yes — you can browse and save recipes on your own. Family features like shared meal planning and the shopping list become available once you create or join a family group.

How do I import a recipe from a website?

Tap the + button in your recipe box and choose "Import from URL." Paste the link to any recipe page and Tutti will extract the details automatically. You can also share directly from Safari or Chrome — tap the Share button in your browser and choose Tutti from the share sheet.
